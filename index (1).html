04 — Research

Featured Research

A Comparative Study of Statistical and Multivariate AR-Net Models for Sector-Level Electricity Demand Forecasting in Sri Lanka Using Macroeconomic and Climatic Variables

National Institute of Business Management (NIBM), in collaboration with Coventry University · 2026
  • Benchmarked five models (ARIMA, ARIMAX, SARIMA, SARIMAX, Multivariate AR-Net) on 300 months of Ceylon Electricity Board data (2000–2024), forecasting domestic and industrial demand separately.
  • Added GDP, population, Industrial Index of Production, temperature and humidity as exogenous variables, cutting domestic MAPE from 17.35% (ARIMA) to 4.34% (AR-Net).
  • Built a dual-language pipeline (R and Python/TensorFlow-Keras) with Box-Cox/log transformations and RobustScaler normalisation to handle the COVID-19 and 2022 crisis structural breaks.
  • Selected features with Granger causality testing and Random Forest + Mutual Information ranking; evaluated all models on an identical 24-month hold-out (2023–2024) with RMSE, MAE, MAPE and Diebold–Mariano tests.
  • Generated three-month-ahead forecasts (January–March 2025) to support short-term planning.
